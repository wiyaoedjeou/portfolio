2025 WEAR & FATIGUE

Effect of the rail surface topography on wear and fatigue

Edjeou W., Larsson P.-O., Larsson R., Almqvist A.

🔍 Key finding: Surface topography significantly drives both wear rate and crack initiation in wheel-rail contact.

This study investigates how rail surface topography — roughness, waviness, and texture — influences the tribological behaviour of the wheel-rail interface. Using a combination of experimental characterisation and numerical modelling, the work establishes quantitative relationships between surface features and fatigue damage indicators, providing a predictive framework for rail maintenance planning.

Tribology International · June 2025

2024 RAIL GRINDING

Evaluating the Impact of Rail Surface Roughness Post-Grinding: An Experimental and Elastoplastic Modelling Approach

Edjeou W., Moström O., Asplund M., Almqvist A.

🔍 Key finding: Grinding-induced roughness concentrates stress and strain, significantly increasing crack initiation risk.

Rail grinding is routinely conducted to prevent crack propagation and surface deterioration. However, grinding introduces surface roughness that can generate stress and strain concentrations. This paper integrates replicated rail roughness into an elastoplastic contact model to assess effects on contact pressure, residual strain and stress distributions.

Proceedings IMechE Part J: Engineering Tribology · 2024

2023 PAVEMENT TRIBOLOGY

Multiscale analysis of the relation between skid resistance and pavement surface texture evolution with polishing

Edjeou W., Cerezo V., Do M.-T., Zahouani H., Ropert C., Augris P.

🔍 Key finding: Texture scales between 0–1000 μm are most relevant for friction evolution during polishing.

This paper develops a multiscale texture decomposition method based on continuous wavelet analysis to identify the relevant spatial scales driving pavement skid resistance variation during polishing. Validated against 3D surface measurements and Wehner–Schulze polishing tests on five aggregate types.

Road Materials and Pavement Design, Vol. 25, No. 1 · 2023

2021 AGGREGATE TEXTURE

A contribution to texture analysis of pavements under simulated polishing: some new findings

Edjeou W., Cerezo V., Zahouani H., Do M.-T.

🔍 Key finding: Wavelet decomposition reveals aggregate-scale texture features controlling long-term skid resistance.

A multiscale texture analysis method is developed and applied to aggregate surfaces to explain road friction variation during polishing. Combines polishing tests, friction measurements, and 3D surface mapping using Alicona. Scales 0–1000 μm proven most significant for friction.

Proceedings of ICR Congress · 2021

2021 SKID RESISTANCE

Embedded estimation of tire/road skid resistance for autonomous vehicle safety

Edjeou W., Cerezo V., Do M.-T.

🔍 Key finding: Real-time water film height estimation enables dynamic skid resistance prediction for autonomous vehicles.

Embedded system for estimating water film height and tire/road skid resistance under wet conditions, for autonomous shuttle safety (Project ENA). Validated through instrumented braking tests, demonstrating reliable real-time estimation integrable in vehicle safety systems.

University Gustave Eiffel / EASE · 2021–2022

2021 PhD THESIS

Analyse multiéchelle de la texture des chaussées — effet sur l'adhérence des revêtements routiers

Edjeou W. — IFSTTAR / Centrale Nantes

🔍 PhD thesis: Full multiscale framework for pavement texture analysis and skid resistance prediction.

Doctoral thesis developing a complete multiscale decomposition method to explain the effect of texture scales on skid resistance evolution with polishing. Integrable into multi-criteria road property optimization. Combines experimental study with a viscoelastic numerical simulation code.

IFSTTAR / Centrale Nantes · 2021
