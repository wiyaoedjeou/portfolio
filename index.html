Who I am

Engineer. Researcher.
Problem solver.

I am a Tribology & Contact Mechanics Engineer with a PhD from IFSTTAR / Centrale Nantes. My work bridges fundamental research and industrial application — from the physics of tire-road friction to rail fatigue and rolling resistance modelling.

Over 5 years, I have worked across 4 countries and sectors — academia, transport infrastructure, autonomous vehicles, and now automotive R&D at Michelin in Clermont-Ferrand, where I develop predictive wear models and digital twins.

I combine deep technical expertise with practical problem-solving, scientific rigour with engineering efficiency. French native, fluent in English (C2), with experience in international teams across France, Sweden, and beyond.
